Clear All System Data ("Start from Scratch")

Reset operational records to start a fresh school term or clean out test data.

Automatic Safety Pre-Wipe Backup Guarantee

Before a single record is cleared, the system will automatically generate and save a complete snapshot of your current database. You will be provided with an immediate download link and can restore your data at any time from the Backup Center!

Data that will be cleared:
  • All registered Students and their credentials (BB IDs)
  • All daily Attendance records and daily lock states
  • All Exam Results (Quiz, Assignment, Final)
  • All Classes, Student-Class enrollments & Timetables
  • All Student Fees, Payments & Clearance Cards
  • All Internal Messages, Announcements & Admissions
Data preserved safely:
  • Administrator Accounts (You will stay logged in)
  • School Profile & Core Settings (School name, motto, email)
  • Color Themes & UI Customizations
  • Standard 3-Part Exam Rules (Quiz/20, Assignment/20, Final/60)
  • All past Backups & Snapshots in the backup archive
Authorized action only. Administrator password and typed confirmation required.
